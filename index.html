Helpful information

Questions parents often ask.

If you have a question not covered here, message us and we’ll talk it through.

Do you offer lessons at home?

Yes. Home-based tuition can be discussed for learners in Nairobi and surrounding areas, depending on location, subject and availability.

Can my child learn online from outside Kenya?

Yes. Online learning can connect learners from different locations. Contact us with the learner’s time zone, curriculum and needs.

Which curriculum do you support?

We support a range of local and international curricula, including CBE/CBC, 8-4-4, Cambridge IGCSE, Pearson Edexcel, GCSE, IB, American Curriculum and ACE. Share your specific programme when enquiring.

How do I enquire about fees and availability?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the learner’s class/year, curriculum, subjects, preferred learning mode and location. We’ll discuss the available options.

Can I submit a review?

Yes. Use the parent review form above. It opens WhatsApp so you can send feedback to our team. Reviews are not published automatically, and we will seek separate permission before publishing.
